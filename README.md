# 🧅 The “Two Onions” Problem

Imagine standing in a vegetable market.

You see **two onions**:

- One is perfectly round and polished.
- The other is slightly misshapen with visible roots.

Like most people, you instinctively choose the flawless one.

But what happens to the second onion?

Even though it is completely edible and nutritious, it gets rejected by the traditional supply chain simply because it does not meet cosmetic standards. Farmers are forced to sell it for pennies — or worse, let it rot in landfills, producing methane emissions and contributing to India’s massive **₹90,000+ Crore annual agricultural waste problem**.

Now think about the farmer behind that crop.

Months of hard work, investment, uncertainty, weather risk, and hope — only to face rejection because of appearance-based grading systems controlled by middlemen.

That rejected produce directly impacts:
- Farmer income
- Family stability
- Education opportunities
- Loan eligibility
- Mental well-being

Many small farmers cannot afford independent transportation to search for better buyers. Traditional banks refuse credit because these farmers lack formal financial records.

# 🤖 1. AI-Powered Produce Grading Engine

### Powered by Groq + Llama-4 Vision

KhetFlow uses advanced Computer Vision to instantly analyze and grade imperfect produce.

Instead of rejecting Grade B & C vegetables, our system intelligently categorizes and lists them directly on the marketplace.

## ✨ Mixed Batch AR Scanner

Farmers simply capture **one image** of a mixed crate.

Our AI:
- Detects every produce item
- Maps X/Y coordinates
- Applies AR overlay markers
- Generates instant inventory classification

### Example:
- 🟢 Grade B
- 🔴 Grade C

This dramatically reduces manual sorting time and increases listing efficiency.

```javascript
// KhetFlow AI Core: Batch Segregation Mapping

  model: "meta-llama/llama-4-scout-17b-16e-instruct",
  messages: [{
    role: "user",
    content: [
      {
        text: "Analyze this crate of mixed produce. Map items with X/Y coordinates for AR overlay rendering."
      },
      {
        image_url: {
          url: capturedFrame

      }
```

# 🚚 2. AI-Driven “Milk Run” Logistics

Transportation is one of the biggest financial burdens for small farmers.

Most farmers cannot afford:
- Individual freight transport
- Long-distance delivery
- Cold-chain logistics

## KhetFlow solves this using AI-optimized transport pooling.

Our system:
- Dynamically groups nearby farmers
- Creates optimized pickup routes
- Aggregates produce efficiently
- Delivers directly to B2B buyers

This creates:
- Lower transportation costs
- Reduced fuel usage
- Faster delivery cycles
- Lower spoilage rates
- Higher farmer profit margins

### 🌱 Result:
A sustainable logistics network built specifically for fragmented agricultural ecosystems.
